# Fullscreen & streaming strategy

## 2026-08-16

- Most apps are not really running fullscreen on the phone.
- Streaming the whole screen may not be the smartest approach for every app.
- Direction: find a way to make it **mobile native**, and keep streaming **only for apps that don’t register** / don’t have a native path.
- Prefer native mobile UI where possible; fall back to stream for apps that can’t be remapped.
